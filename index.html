Case Studies

Case studies: frameworks in practice.

A set of product case studies, each working through a real problem end to end (diagnosis, prioritisation, and a defined solution), using structured PM frameworks throughout: JTBD, First Principles, AARRR, RICE, Nielsen's Heuristics, PRD writing, funnel analysis, cohort analysis and system design.

Cult.fit · Case Study

From Intent to Habit: Increasing Routine Activation

Only 34% of new Cultpass users build a lasting workout routine within their first 45 days. Diagnosed the drop-off through 20 semi-structured user interviews and a full funnel breakdown, using JTBD and First Principles to reframe the problem: users aren't short on motivation, they're short on a starting path and a way back in after a missed session. Prioritised solutions with RICE and shortlisted two: Cult Base Camp, which auto-schedules a user's first week to remove decision fatigue, and Cult Reboot, a guilt-free comeback flow that rebuilds momentum after a missed streak instead of penalising it. Modelled to move activation from 34% to 50%, backed by a phased Bangalore-first rollout plan and a defined North Star metric.

Zomato · Case Study

Funnel Optimisation and Retention Strategy

Mapped Zomato's end-to-end delivery funnel across 7 stages and identified cart abandonment due to surprise fees and weak post-delivery re-engagement as highest-leverage drop-off points. Defined three user cohorts (new users, high vs low frequency, and discount-driven vs full-price) and built North Star and ladder metric frameworks with input, output, leading and lagging indicators per hypothesis.
