Method

Four steps.
One build.

Ninety minutes to decide, one page to act on. No jargon, no slide deck — a plan a small team can execute from tomorrow.

STEP 01

Framing.

We name the central question.

Objective, context, constraints, stakeholders, urgency. We separate the symptom from the real problem — and name what nobody's said out loud.

Format
60–90 min, remote or onsite
Deliverable
Structured brief, shared live
Outputs
Objective, scope, constraints
STEP 02

Diagnosis.

We look under the hood.

Architecture, org, process, decisions, resourcing, debt, alignment. The real blockers are rarely the stated ones — we surface them one by one.

Format
Targeted reviews + technical read
Deliverable
Prioritized blocker map
Outputs
Top 3 frictions, risks, pending calls
STEP 03

Prioritization.

Three priorities. Not ten.

We cut between what matters now and what can wait. RICE, MoSCoW, or plain judgment, depending on context — trade-offs stated out loud.

Methods
RICE · MoSCoW · Cost of Delay
Deliverable
Top 3 + stop-doing list
Outputs
Explicit sort criteria, trade-offs
STEP 04

Shipping.

Guardrails, not afterthoughts.

Launch with the autonomy tier, safety triggers, and audit trail the use case actually needs — defined before build, not bolted on after.

Format
Launch plan, one page
Deliverable
Autonomy tier, triggers, audit trail
Outputs
Success criteria, review date
